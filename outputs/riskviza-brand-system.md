# Riskviza Brand System

Date: 2026-07-08

## Brand Decision

Public brand: **Riskviza**

Product family:

- Riskviza Register
- Riskviza Evidence
- Riskviza Vendors

Positioning:

Riskviza is the EU-native risk intelligence platform that connects cyber, vendor, AI, ESG, and operational risk into one evidence-backed view for boards and risk teams.

## Brand Personality

Riskviza should feel:

- Board-ready
- Calm under pressure
- EU-native
- Evidence-backed
- Modern and technical, but not flashy
- Premium SaaS, not consulting brochure

Avoid:

- Generic blue cybersecurity styling
- Shield and lock cliches
- Heavy enterprise GRC text density
- Loud neon compliance visuals
- Dark-only cyber aesthetic

## Logo Concept

The logo uses a connected **R** mark with a diagonal signal path. The four nodes represent risk domains coming together:

- Cyber
- Vendor
- AI / automation
- ESG and operational risk

The diagonal line represents Riskviza turning fragmented signals into board-ready decisions.

## Color System

Primary:

- Ink: `#071B2C`
- Paper: `#F8FAF8`
- Mist: `#EEF4F2`

Signal colors:

- Teal: `#38D5B5`
- Deep teal: `#0D9B87`
- Violet: `#7C3DF2`
- Soft violet: `#EFE7FF`
- Amber: `#F5B84B`

Functional:

- Success: `#20C878`
- Danger: `#E94F4F`
- Slate text: `#5C6B78`
- Muted text: `#87949D`
- Line: `#DBE5E2`

Use color carefully:

- Ink and paper should do most of the work.
- Teal is the main action/status signal.
- Violet is for intelligence, AI, mapping, and automation.
- Amber is for attention, not decoration.
- Avoid one-note blue or purple dominance.

## Typography

Recommended fonts:

- Display: Manrope
- UI/body: Inter

Type direction:

- Use strong, readable SaaS typography.
- Keep section copy short.
- Prefer visual proof, charts, and product UI over long paragraphs.
- Use large stats for trust and outcomes.

Suggested scale:

- Hero: 64-76px desktop, 42-48px mobile
- H2: 42-52px desktop, 32-38px mobile
- H3: 24-30px
- Body: 16-18px
- UI labels: 12-14px

## UI Style

Surfaces:

- Light base surface.
- Off-white or mist-tinted bands for major sections.
- Deep ink section only for proof/trust moments, used sparingly.

Cards and panels:

- Border radius: 8-14px for product UI, 14-22px for large marketing panels.
- Avoid overly pill-shaped cards except segmented controls and CTAs.
- Use subtle borders first, shadows second.

Charts:

- Bar charts for risk by domain.
- Donut charts for evidence completion and vendor assurance.
- Line charts for risk trend over time.
- Matrix/table views for framework mapping.
- Scorecards for board-readiness, vendor criticality, and evidence confidence.

Interaction:

- Subtle card lift on hover.
- Segmented tabs for pain-point explorer.
- Animated control/framework mapping visual.
- CTA arrow capsule interaction inspired by Vanta, but in Riskviza colors.

## Homepage Design Requirements

Hero:

- Clear Riskviza logo and favicon.
- Headline should mention board-ready risk intelligence.
- Must show a real-looking product UI visual above the fold.
- Include chart/stat/product screenshot elements, not abstract decoration.

Key sections:

1. Hero with board-ready risk dashboard.
2. Segmented pain-point explorer.
3. Three product modules: Register, Evidence, Vendors.
4. Animated framework/control mapping visual.
5. Board report preview.
6. EU regulatory alignment.
7. Persona/use-case section.
8. Request demo form.
